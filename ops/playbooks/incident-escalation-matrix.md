# Anchora Incident Escalation Matrix

## Escalation Chain

### P1 Critical
1. Incident Commander (immediate)
2. CISO and CTO (within 15 minutes)
3. CEO and General Counsel (within 30 minutes)
4. Head of Communications (within 30 minutes)

### P2 High
1. Incident Commander
2. CISO and service owner (within 30 minutes)
3. Legal and compliance representative (within 2 hours)

### P3 Medium
1. Service owner
2. Security operations lead
3. Incident Commander on call if unresolved after 2 hours

### P4 Low
1. Service owner or helpdesk
2. Security operations queue if recurring pattern emerges

## Role Responsibilities
- Incident Commander: owns operational decisions and timeline
- Tech Lead: owns containment and recovery execution
- Legal Lead: owns regulatory and contractual obligations
- Comms Lead: owns stakeholder updates
- Scribe: owns incident log and evidence package

## Notification Cadence
- P1: every 30 minutes
- P2: every 60 minutes
- P3: every 4 hours
- P4: end-of-day summary if needed

## Escalation Triggers
- Customer impact exceeds 15 minutes for Tier-1 services
- Confirmed unauthorized data access
- Failed restore attempt
- Repeat incident pattern within 7 days
